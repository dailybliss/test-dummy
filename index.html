Everything about creating your marriage biodata

A marriage biodata (also called a matrimonial biodata or shaadi biodata) is a personal profile used in the Indian matrimonial process. Unlike a professional resume, a marriage biodata includes personal details like date of birth, height, religion, caste, gotra, family background, horoscope details, and partner preferences — information relevant to finding a compatible life partner. It's shared with prospective families during the marriage search process.
