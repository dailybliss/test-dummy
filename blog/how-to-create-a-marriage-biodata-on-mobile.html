What Is a Marriage Biodata?

A marriage biodata is a personal profile prepared for matrimonial purposes. It generally contains information about a person's personal background, education, profession, family and other details that may be relevant when looking for a prospective life partner.

Depending on personal preferences, family traditions and community requirements, a marriage biodata may include your name, date of birth, age, height, education, profession, family details, location, hobbies, interests, partner preferences and contact information.

A marriage biodata can be created in several formats, including a professionally designed PDF, an image or a simple text-based profile that can be shared through messaging apps.

What Information Should You Include in a Marriage Biodata?

Before you start creating your biodata on your phone, it is helpful to keep the required information ready. A typical marriage biodata may include:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth or age
  • Height
  • Religion or community, if relevant
  • Mother tongue
  • Current city or location
  • Educational qualifications
  • Profession, job or business details
  • Family information
  • Hobbies and interests
  • A brief personal introduction
  • Partner preferences
  • Contact information
  • A recent photograph

You do not need to include every possible detail. It is better to provide relevant and accurate information that gives a clear introduction while avoiding unnecessary or sensitive personal information.

Tips for Creating a Professional Marriage Biodata on Mobile

1. Use a Recent Photograph

Choose a clear and recent photograph with good lighting. Avoid using overly edited photographs, group pictures or images in which your face is difficult to see.

2. Keep the Biodata Easy to Read

A marriage biodata should be easy to scan. Use clear headings, short sections and sufficient spacing. Avoid adding excessive information simply to make the biodata longer.

3. Check All Information Carefully

Before sharing your biodata, verify your name, date of birth, education, profession, family details and contact information. Correct spelling and accurate information can make the profile more professional.

4. Select an Appropriate Design

Choose a design that matches your personality and preferences. A simple layout can provide a clean and modern appearance, while traditional or decorative designs may be preferred for certain matrimonial profiles.

5. Avoid Sharing Sensitive Information

A marriage biodata generally does not require sensitive information such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, passport details, bank account information, passwords or confidential financial documents. Share only the information that is necessary for your matrimonial profile.

6. Keep the Information Genuine

Your marriage biodata should provide an honest introduction. Keep information about your education, profession, hobbies, family and partner preferences accurate and straightforward.
