Tips for a Professional Marriage Biodata

  • Use a clear and professional layout.
  • Add a good-quality photograph.
  • Keep personal and professional information accurate.
  • Use consistent fonts and formatting.
  • Avoid filling the page with unnecessary information.
  • Proofread the biodata before sharing it.
  • Save the final document as PDF if you want to preserve its formatting.
