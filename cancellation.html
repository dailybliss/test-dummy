Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Important: Marriage Biodata Maker is a pay-per-download digital service with no subscriptions. There is nothing to cancel — you pay once per download session and receive immediate access.

1. No Subscription Model

Marriage Biodata Maker does not operate on a subscription basis. There are no recurring charges, no monthly fees, and no automatic renewals. You make a one-time payment only when you choose to download a premium biodata template. You are never automatically charged.

2. Pre-Payment Cancellation

You can cancel a payment at any time before it is completed by:

  • Closing the Razorpay payment window without completing the transaction
  • Clicking "Cancel" or "Go Back" within the Razorpay checkout screen

No charge is applied if you close the payment window before completing the transaction. You will only be charged when the payment is explicitly confirmed and completed.

3. Post-Payment Cancellation

Since our service delivers a digital product (biodata download) immediately upon successful payment, post-payment cancellation is not applicable in the traditional sense. Once a payment is completed and the download is made available, the transaction is considered fulfilled.

If you have paid but have not yet downloaded your biodata, please refer to our Refund Policy for applicable circumstances.

4. Failed Payment

If your payment fails or is declined during the checkout process, no amount is charged. You are free to retry with a different payment method. Failed payment transactions are automatically voided.

5. Free Templates

Free templates do not require any payment. They can be used and downloaded at any time without any commitment or charges.

6. Contact Us

If you have any concerns about a charge or need assistance, please contact us at admin@marriagebiodatamaker.co and we will resolve the matter promptly.
