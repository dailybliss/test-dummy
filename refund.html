Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our commitment: We want you to be satisfied with your biodata. If you experience a technical issue that prevents you from downloading your biodata after payment, we will make it right.

1. Nature of Our Service

Marriage Biodata Maker provides a digital download service. Upon successful payment, you receive immediate access to download your biodata as a PDF and/or image file. Because the service is delivered digitally and instantly, our refund policy reflects this nature.

2. Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a full refund in the following circumstances:

  • Your payment was successfully processed but the download did not work due to a technical error on our end
  • You were charged multiple times for the same download session
  • The downloaded file was corrupted or empty due to a technical issue

3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:

  • You successfully downloaded the biodata but changed your mind about the design
  • You selected a paid template when a free template was available
  • You were unable to download due to your own browser, device, or internet issues
  • You did not read the preview before making payment (a full live preview is available before purchase)
  • Requests made more than 7 days after the transaction date

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at admin@marriagebiodatamaker.co within 7 days of your transaction with:

  • Your Razorpay Payment ID (found in your payment confirmation email)
  • A description of the issue you experienced
  • Screenshots if applicable

We will review your request and respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

5. Free Templates

No payment is required for free templates. Therefore, no refund is applicable for free template downloads.

6. Contact

For refund requests or payment queries, contact us at admin@marriagebiodatamaker.co.
